# Debate rounds

Complete [Research.md](Research.md) and write the member briefs before Round 1. Use independent calls when available and permitted, or the disclosed single-session simulation in SKILL.md. Each round gets its own table.

| Round | Member's task | Required context |
|---|---|---|
| 1: Initial positions | Explain a position, the supporting evidence, and its main condition or risk | Brief, topic, user constraints, relevant research findings |
| 2: Responses and challenges | Address another member by name and examine a specific point from Round 1 | The same inputs plus the completed Round 1 table |
| 3: Synthesis | Identify agreement, unresolved disagreement, and a final recommendation | The same inputs plus completed Round 1 and Round 2 tables |

Members within a round may run concurrently. Rounds themselves run sequentially. Give later rounds the final, humanized Indonesian tables so the transcript matches what members are responding to.

Keep evidence and disagreement visible. A changed position should say which finding or argument changed it. Do not force consensus or manufacture disagreement for drama.

Apply [Humanize.md](Humanize.md), [GenZVoice.md](GenZVoice.md), and the final evidence check to every response and the overall synthesis. Use [OutputFormat.md](OutputFormat.md) for table schemas and detail requirements. Runtime depends on the host, browsing, and number of agent calls; give no fixed completion guarantee.
