# Humanizer for council

Use the complete bundled [Humanizer 3.1.0](references/humanizer.md) in embedded mode. This guide connects that skill to the council workflow. The source's [MIT license](references/humanizer.LICENSE) ships with the package.

## Apply to every original prose cell

Edit research summaries, source-access notes, roster descriptions, every member's response in every round, and all synthesis or summary rows. Keep table schemas, citations, identifiers, and direct quotations intact.

1. Draft from the research findings and the member's brief. Separate sourced facts from judgments and unresolved questions.
2. Run Humanizer's full process: identify patterns, rewrite, check the draft against the source, and produce the final wording. Preserve the member's speaking habits.
3. Apply [GenZVoice.md](GenZVoice.md) to the cleaned text.
4. Run a final Humanizer check on the styled text. Slang must not add canned openers, dramatic closers, or repetitive sentence patterns.
5. Compare with the evidence again. Verify names, numbers, dates, units, citations, conditions, and uncertainty. Keep dissent that affects the recommendation.
6. Put only the final Indonesian wording into the table.

Do not invent a citation, personal experience, or degree of certainty to make a response sound more human. Do not weaken a documented risk to keep the tone casual. Direct quotations retain their original wording and attribution.

## Reviewer's voice

Keep the brief's sentence rhythm and way of reasoning across rounds. A builder may focus on the smallest workable implementation; an analyst may keep returning to the source's scope. Both speak naturally without repeating the same catchphrase in every row.

The same process applies when a single session simulates the reviewers. Show the execution limitation in the research table and keep the voices separate without claiming independent agreement.
