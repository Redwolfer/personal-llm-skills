# DEBATE workflow

Use three rounds to examine the decision. Read [Research.md](../Research.md), [CouncilMembers.md](../CouncilMembers.md), [RoundStructure.md](../RoundStructure.md), and [OutputFormat.md](../OutputFormat.md).

## 1. Research

Identify the topic and constraints, then search across source types before writing positions. Attempt primary sources, Reddit, X/Twitter, and other relevant sites as Research.md describes. Read the supporting pages, record source-access gaps, and declare independent-agent or simulated execution in the research table.

## 2. Brief members

Write four topic-specific briefs in English with different analytical roles, research inputs, initial stances, and speaking habits. The public roster is Indonesian. Do not invent personal experience or claim real experts participated.

## 3. Run Round 1

Each member states a position, supporting findings, and its key concern. Supply its brief, the question, user constraints, and relevant research rows. Aim for 150-220 words per position, with at least 150 unless the user requests a shorter format.

Run independent agents when available and permitted; otherwise use separate perspective drafts in the current session. Follow the same choice for the remaining rounds and disclose any change in mode.

## 4. Run Round 2

Wait for all Round 1 responses to pass the output pipeline. Give each member the completed Round 1 table and its original inputs. Require a response to another named member's specific point. Examine the assumption or add evidence; do not repeat opening positions. Aim for 150-220 words per response.

## 5. Run Round 3

Give each member the completed Round 1 and Round 2 tables with its original inputs. Ask for agreement, remaining disagreement, and a final recommendation. Aim for 150-220 words in the recommendation cell; supporting agreement cells can be shorter. Explain any change of position.

## 6. Synthesize and deliver

Write the overall synthesis from all rounds and the research: areas of agreement, remaining disagreement, evidence strength, and a concrete recommendation. Keep each row developed, normally 120-200 words, with the minimum in OutputFormat.md unless the user requests less detail.

For every member response, research summary, roster description, limitation, and synthesis row, apply [Humanize.md](../Humanize.md), [GenZVoice.md](../GenZVoice.md), and the final evidence check before publication. The complete Humanizer is bundled at [references/humanizer.md](../references/humanizer.md); use embedded mode and include only final wording.

Assemble the topic heading, research table, roster, three separate round tables, and synthesis in that order. Use the Indonesian schemas in OutputFormat.md. End at the last synthesis row without an opening speech or closing offer.
